Why Upvotes Beget Upvotes

Social influence bias & the herding of crowds — what the randomized experiments actually show.

You post a comment. Someone secretly gifts it one upvote before anyone else sees it. Does that single vote change its fate? You can’t tell by watching a live feed — popular things are popular partly because they are good. So researchers planted votes at random, on identical content, and watched.

The answer: yes. That one seeded upvote made the next viewer 32% more likely to upvote too, and the nudge snowballed into a 25% higher final score. But the effect is lopsided — the crowd corrects fake downvotes and lets fake upvotes ride — and quality still guards the extremes: the best content rarely fails outright, the worst rarely wins. Social influence rules the vast, path-dependent middle.

What the Research Shows

  • 01 Upvotes beget upvotes. One planted upvote made the first viewer 32% more likely to upvote too (P = 1.0×10⁻⁶) — and over five months, snowballed into a 25% higher final score. Muchnik, Aral & Taylor 2013, Science.
  • 02 The crowd un-downvotes you. Planted downvotes got upvoted more as the crowd rushed to correct them (0.099 vs 0.054); no significant negative herding anywhere. Praise compounds; unfair criticism rebounds.
  • 03 Popularity signals warp markets. Show people the download counts and hits get bigger and far less predictable — the same song was a smash in one world and a flop in another. Salganik, Dodds & Watts 2006, Science.
  • 04 Fake popularity partly comes true. Invert the displayed counts and most songs track their false rank — yet the genuinely best songs climbed back. Quality still guards the extremes. Salganik & Watts 2008.
  • 05 It steers attention, not taste. Popularity changed which songs people sampled, not their judgment after listening — and the count’s grip tightened over time. Krumme et al. 2012.
  • 06 Mind the limits. Single platforms, small absolute baselines, a pre-algorithmic era — and the asymmetry may not travel: on Reddit, random downvotes did herd downward.
